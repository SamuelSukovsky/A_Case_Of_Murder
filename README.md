# A_Case_Of_Murder

## How to Play
WASD - movement
Shift - sprint
Mouse - look around
Left Mouse Button - interact
Right Mouse Button - zoom/inspect item
E - highlight interactable items

To complete the game, the player is required to locate two specific items within the office and pin them on the evidence board. The clues to these may be found using the computer stationed near the window.
